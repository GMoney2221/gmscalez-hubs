About

Chases Mobile Mechanic is an ASE Certified mobile mechanic serving Mesa, Tempe, Chandler, Gilbert, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Queen Creek and the rest of the East Valley. Chase and his team come to your home, work or wherever the vehicle is parked, so there is no tow and no shop wait. With over 5 years of experience, he handles brakes, suspension and steering, check engine light and no-start diagnostics, batteries, alternators, starters, electrical problems, engine work, cooling system work, oil changes, pre-purchase inspections, motor swaps, transmission swaps and transmission flushes. Chase does not do A/C work or transmission rebuilds, and does not service European or luxury vehicles.
